# About Catenda AI Connect for Claude

> Catenda AI Connect for Claude lets a team ask Claude about its Catenda Hub projects, check project data and hand over routine work. What it is, what an organization prepares and how to get started.

> **Important:** Catenda AI Connect is in early access. Features and setup steps change often, so what appears on screen can differ from this article.

> **Note:** This article is for signed-out readers. Links to all three levels of this topic are at the end of this article.

Many teams already use Claude, the AI app made by Anthropic, for writing, research and analysis. Catenda AI Connect brings their Catenda Hub projects into that same Claude. Questions about a project are asked in plain language and answered from the live models, documents and topics. Where the organization allows Claude to make changes, the findings also become topics in Catenda Hub.

## 1. **What it is**

Claude connects to Catenda Hub through the Catenda connector. The connector uses MCP (the Model Context Protocol), an open standard that Claude supports for connecting to other systems.

Teams keep working in the Claude they know, and Catenda Hub stays the single source of project information. Everything Claude creates or changes is recorded in Catenda Hub, like any other coordination work.

## 2. **What teams use it for**

### 2.1 **Ask**

Questions are answered from the live project data, with sources. For example:

```
What changed on the project this week, and which topics are overdue?
```

### 2.2 **Check**

Topics, models and documents are checked against the project's requirements. They can relate to deliveries or any other task. For example:

```
Check this delivery against our BIM Execution Plan and list what fails.
```

### 2.3 **Delegate**

Structured busywork is done as drafts a person approves. For example:

```
Turn these meeting minutes into topics, each with an owner and a deadline.
```

## 3. **Requirements**

### 3.1 **In an organization**

An organization uses Catenda AI Connect on a Claude Team or Enterprise plan. An owner of that Claude organization manages connectors for everyone in it. Claude plans are bought from Anthropic, on its website.

### 3.2 **On a personal account**

Catenda AI Connect also works on a personal Claude Pro or Max account. A personal account connects only when the organization has not asked Catenda to limit Catenda AI Connect to users on its own domain. A personal account follows its own Claude settings, not the organization's. That is why the organization's AI policy should say whether personal accounts may be used for work data.

## 4. **Preparing the organization**

A few things are decided once, by the organization, before a team starts.

### 4.1 **One connector for everyone**

An owner adds the Catenda connector to the organization's Claude once. It then becomes available to the members, so nobody sets it up on their own. On an Enterprise plan, the owner can also give it to some groups only.

## 5. **Access and control**

The organization decides what Claude may do in Catenda Hub, and each member keeps their own access.

### 5.1 **What Claude may do**

The organization decides which kinds of actions Claude may take in Catenda Hub: reading, writing or deleting. Each kind can be allowed, set to ask for approval first, or blocked. Access can also be set per feature, for example so Claude may create topics but only reads everything else. An organization that only wants answers can keep Claude to reading. Members can make these settings stricter for themselves.

### 5.2 **Each member's own access**

Each member signs in to the connector with their own Catenda account. Claude then works with exactly the access that member has in Catenda Hub. The project's permissions apply unchanged, and access rights stay managed in one place.

### 5.3 **Access to documents**

Claude opens and reads a document in a protected workspace of its own. That workspace reaches only the internet addresses the organization allows. So for Claude to read documents from Catenda Hub, an owner allows network access to Catenda in the organization's Claude settings.

## 6. **Getting started**

Catenda AI Connect is available in early access.

Catenda AI Connect is not only for organizations that own projects in Catenda Hub. An organization that is invited to the projects of other organizations can get it too. [Catenda AI Connect and project access](https://support.catenda.com/en/articles/17778745-catenda-ai-connect-and-project-access) describes this in more detail.

Catenda AI Connect also works with other AI apps, as described in [About Catenda AI Connect](https://support.catenda.com/en/articles/17778695-about-catenda-ai-connect).
