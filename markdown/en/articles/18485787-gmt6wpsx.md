# About Catenda AI Connect for ChatGPT

> Catenda AI Connect brings Catenda Hub project data into ChatGPT. What teams use it for, what an organization looks into before it starts, and how to get started.

> **Important:** Catenda AI Connect is in early access. Features and setup steps change often, so what appears on screen can differ from this article.

Catenda AI Connect connects ChatGPT to the models, documents and topics in Catenda Hub. Questions about a project are asked in plain language in ChatGPT and answered from the live project data. Where the organization allows ChatGPT to make changes, the findings also become topics in Catenda Hub.

This article describes what teams do with it and what an organization looks into before it starts.

## 1. **What teams use it for**

### 1.1 **Ask**

Questions are answered from the live project data, with sources. For example:

```
What happened on the project this week? What is overdue? Where is the risk?
```

### 1.2 **Check**

Topics, models and documents are checked against the project's requirements. They can relate to deliveries or any other task. For example:

```
Check the naming and metadata of this delivery against our BIM Execution Plan. Flag what fails.
```

### 1.3 **Delegate**

Structured busywork is done as drafts a person approves. For example:

```
The client sent this spreadsheet. Create a topic per line, set owner and deadline.
```

## 2. **Requirements**

### 2.1 **In an organization**

The workspace needs ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Education (ChatGPT Edu, the plan for educational institutions). ChatGPT plans are bought from OpenAI, on its website.

### 2.2 **On a personal account**

Catenda AI Connect also works on a personal ChatGPT Plus or Pro account, where the user adds it as a custom plugin. A personal account connects only when the organization has not asked Catenda to limit Catenda AI Connect to users on its own domain. A personal account follows the user's own ChatGPT settings, not the organization's. That is why the organization's AI policy should say whether personal accounts may be used for work data.

## 3. **What the organization needs**

Catenda AI Connect is added to ChatGPT by the organization, once, as a custom plugin. The members it is made available to then use it in their own ChatGPT.

This is what the Catenda AI Connect plugin can look like in ChatGPT:

![The Catenda AI Connect plugin page in ChatGPT with the Manage and Try now buttons](https://catenda.github.io/help-center-ops/images/gmt6wpsx/01-what-the-organization-needs.png)

### 3.1 **Workspace admin**

The plugin is added and published by an Admin or Owner of the ChatGPT workspace. This is usually the IT department or whoever manages AI apps for the organization.

### 3.2 **Who gets it**

On Business, a published plugin is available to every member of the workspace. On Enterprise and Education, it can be given to specific groups through custom roles.

### 3.3 **Keeping it current**

ChatGPT saves what the plugin offers when it is published, and does not update that by itself. When Catenda AI Connect gains new capabilities, a workspace admin refreshes the plugin to make them available.

## 4. **How each user connects**

Each member connects ChatGPT to their own Catenda account, by signing in to Catenda. ChatGPT then works as that signed-in user, with exactly the access that user has in Catenda Hub. The project's permissions apply unchanged.

## 5. **Control over actions**

The organization decides which kinds of actions ChatGPT may take in Catenda Hub. When the workspace admin publishes the plugin, the admin chooses which of its actions are turned on. Actions that change or delete data can be left off for a workspace that should only read. Access can also be set per feature, for example so ChatGPT may create topics but only reads everything else.

The admin also sets a default for when ChatGPT asks before it acts. Members can change that setting themselves, so the organization's AI policy should say which setting they may use.

## 6. **Getting started**

Catenda AI Connect is available in early access.

Catenda AI Connect is not only for organizations that own projects in Catenda Hub. An organization that is invited to the projects of other organizations can get Catenda AI Connect too. Catenda AI Connect then works on the projects the organization runs itself, and on the projects it is invited to, as long as the organization running that project allows Catenda AI Connect there. [Catenda AI Connect and project access](https://support.catenda.com/en/articles/17778745-catenda-ai-connect-and-project-access) describes this in more detail.
