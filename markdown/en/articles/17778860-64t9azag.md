# About Catenda for Power BI (On Demand)

> Catenda for Power BI brings Catenda Hub project data into Power BI. A next version, with visuals and a ready-made report template, is available on demand.

Catenda for Power BI brings the data of a Catenda Hub project into Microsoft Power BI, for reports on the project's models, documents and topics. The released [Catenda Power BI Connector](https://support.catenda.com/en/articles/5784721-catenda-power-bi-connector) is available to every Catenda customer. A next version is available on demand: it can be requested ahead of its release to everyone.

## 1. **What it is**

The next version comes with a new connector that brings far more of a project's data into Power BI, visuals that show a project's models and topics inside a report, and a ready-made report template. It is still changing a lot, so it is offered for trying out rather than for reports that need to last.

## 2. **Getting started**

Catenda for Power BI works with projects in Catenda Hub. To use it, contact Catenda sales about getting started with a first project: [https://catenda.com/get-started](https://catenda.com/get-started) or [sales@catenda.com](mailto:sales@catenda.com).

> **Note:** More is available to Catenda customers. Sign in to Catenda at [https://hub.catenda.com](https://hub.catenda.com), then open or reload a page of the Help Center. The Help Center then shows more to signed-in customers, starting with [Introduction to Catenda for Power BI (On Demand)](https://support.catenda.com/en/articles/17778865-introduction-to-catenda-for-power-bi-on-demand) _(signed-in customers only)_.
