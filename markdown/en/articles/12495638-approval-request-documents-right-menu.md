# Approval request documents right menu

This article contains information about the following topics: _[Under review](#h_b398f04f49) - [Step opverview](#h_be2d5b96e1)_The right menu can be opened by selecting documents in the [documents tab](https://support.catenda.com/en/articles/12495130-documents-tab-in-an-approval-request) on an approval request page. Depending on our your selecting one documents our multiple the menu look different.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/catenda/help-center/main/images/dmdyh23e/01-intro.png)

### Multiple selection:

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/catenda/help-center/main/images/dmdyh23e/02-multiple-selection.png)

When you select multiple documents you get the possibility to direct review your selection by clicking 'review documents' button.

### Single selection:

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/catenda/help-center/main/images/dmdyh23e/03-single-selection-br.png)

Single selection opens a overview in the right menu. It is the same menu as opens in the document-review in de 2D viewer, but without showing the document visual. It gives reviewers the possibility to approve / reject or submit the document without opening it.

### Steps overview

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/catenda/help-center/main/images/dmdyh23e/04-steps-overview.png)

This is what the step overview can look like where a member of a reviewer team submitted an indication that the document should be approved on behalf of a reviewer team. It shows the step status and the team and member that did the review and submission.
