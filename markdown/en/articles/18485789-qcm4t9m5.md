# About Catenda AI Connect for Gemini

> Catenda AI Connect brings Catenda Hub project data into Gemini. What teams use it for, what the organization needs before it starts, and how to get started.

> **Important:** Catenda AI Connect is in early access. Features and setup steps change often, so what appears on screen can differ from this article.

> **Note:** This article is for signed-out readers. Links to all three levels of this topic are at the end of this article.

Catenda AI Connect connects Gemini to the models, documents and topics in Catenda Hub. Questions about a project are asked in plain language in Gemini and answered from the live project data. Where the organization allows Gemini to make changes, the findings also become topics in Catenda Hub.

This article describes what teams do with it and what the organization needs before it starts.

## 1. **What teams use it for**

### 1.1 **Ask**

Questions are answered from the live project data, with sources. For example:

```
What happened on the project this week? What is overdue? Where is the risk?
```

### 1.2 **Check**

Topics, models and documents are checked against the project's requirements. They can relate to deliveries or any other task. For example:

```
Check the naming and metadata of this delivery against our BIM Execution Plan. Flag what fails.
```

### 1.3 **Delegate**

Structured busywork is done as drafts a person approves. For example:

```
The client sent this spreadsheet. Create a topic per line, set owner and deadline.
```

## 2. **Requirements**

### 2.1 **In an organization**

The organization needs Gemini Enterprise, a separate Google product. The Business edition is the easiest way to start. Its users work at business.gemini.google. Gemini Enterprise also comes as the Standard, Plus or Frontline edition, through Google Cloud. That edition has its own address, which the administrator shares with the users. Custom apps such as Catenda AI Connect are added in Gemini Enterprise, a different product from the Gemini that comes with a Google Workspace plan. Users then work in Gemini Enterprise, not in the Gemini app at gemini.google.com. Gemini Enterprise is bought from Google, on its website.

### 2.2 **On a personal account**

Catenda AI Connect also works on a personal Google account, where the user adds it as a custom app in Gemini. A Google account managed by an organization through Google Workspace cannot add it that way. A personal account connects only when the organization has not asked Catenda to limit Catenda AI Connect to users on its own domain. A personal account follows the user's own Gemini settings, not the organization's. That is why the organization's AI policy should say whether personal accounts may be used for work data.

## 3. **What the organization needs**

Catenda AI Connect is added to Gemini by the organization, once, through Gemini Enterprise. The users it is made available to then use it in their own Gemini.

### 3.1 **Administrator**

Catenda AI Connect is added by an administrator of the organization's Gemini Enterprise. In the Business edition, this is an administrator of the team. Through Google Cloud, it is an administrator of the Google Cloud project that Gemini Enterprise runs in. This is usually the IT department or whoever manages AI apps for the organization. The administrator also chooses what Gemini may do in Catenda Hub, per feature. For example, Gemini may create topics but only read everything else. An organization that should only read can leave changing and deleting off.

## 4. **How each user connects**

Each user links Gemini to their own Catenda account, by signing in to Catenda. Gemini then works as that signed-in user, with exactly the access that user has in Catenda Hub. The project's permissions apply unchanged.

Once connected, Catenda AI Connect is picked in a chat by typing **@**. This is what it can look like:

## 5. **Getting started**

Catenda AI Connect is available in early access.

Catenda AI Connect is not only for organizations that own projects in Catenda Hub. An organization that is invited to the projects of other organizations can get Catenda AI Connect too. Catenda AI Connect then works on the projects the organization runs itself, and on the projects it is invited to, as long as the organization running that project allows Catenda AI Connect there. [Catenda AI Connect and project access](https://support.catenda.com/en/articles/17778745-catenda-ai-connect-and-project-access) describes this in more detail.

To start using Catenda AI Connect in Gemini, contact Catenda sales: [https://catenda.com/get-started](https://catenda.com/get-started) or [sales@catenda.com](mailto:sales@catenda.com).
