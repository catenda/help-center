# Changing the preferred language

The preferred language can be changed on the [preferences page](https://support.catenda.com/en/articles/6880968-account-page#h_87963579a7) of an [account](https://support.catenda.com/en/articles/6880968-account-page).

[YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/embed/fwQtOwQsTN8?rel=0)

## 1. **1. Finding the language setting**

`Profile->Account->Preferences`

The account page can always be found by clicking on the profile picture on the top right and clicking on account in the menu that opens up. Then, in the left navigation menu, find the [preference page](https://support.catenda.com/en/articles/6880968-account-page#h_87963579a7) where the language setting can be found.
