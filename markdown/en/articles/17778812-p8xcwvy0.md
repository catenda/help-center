# About the Catenda Archiving Portal

> The Catenda Archiving Portal lets people transfer the documents and topics they choose from Catenda Hub to their organization's archive system.

The Catenda Archiving Portal lets people transfer the documents and topics they choose from Catenda Hub projects to their organization's own archive system, so the project's records end up where the organization keeps them. It is a separate product next to Catenda Hub.

## 1. **What it is**

Archiving runs in one direction, from Catenda Hub out to the archive system. The portal can submit to archive systems such as WebSak+ from Acos.

## 2. **Getting started**

The Archiving Portal works with projects in Catenda Hub. To use it, contact Catenda sales about getting started with a first project: [https://catenda.com/get-started](https://catenda.com/get-started) or [sales@catenda.com](mailto:sales@catenda.com).
