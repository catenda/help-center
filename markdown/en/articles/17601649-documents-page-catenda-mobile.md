# Documents page - Catenda Mobile

> Browse and open the documents of your project in Catenda Mobile.

The Documents page is opened by tapping **Docs** in the bar at the bottom of the screen, after a project is opened on the [projects page](https://support.catenda.com/en/articles/17601629-projects-page-catenda-mobile).

The Documents page shows the documents of the project in their folders.

![The Documents page with the Labels and Scope filters and the folder list](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/catenda/help-center/main/images/0mhxhsb0/01-intro.jpg)

## 1. **Search documents**

The search button at the top right searches the documents.

## 2. **Filter documents**

The filters under the title choose which documents are listed.

### 2.1 **Labels**

**Labels** shows only the documents with the selected labels.

### 2.2 **Scope**

**Scope** switches between the published and the shared documents of the project.

## 3. **Upload a document**

Documents and photos are uploaded to the project with **+** at the bottom right.

## 4. **The document list**

The list shows the folders and documents of the project. Each document shows its name and file type.

![A list of documents with their file types and a menu button next to each](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/catenda/help-center/main/images/0mhxhsb0/02-the-document-list.png)

Tapping a folder opens it and lists the documents in that folder, and tapping a document opens it. **…** next to a document offers **Open externally** to open it in another app, **Share**, and **Add location** to tie it to a location.

## 5. **Open a document**

An open document shows its name at the top, next to a share button and **…**. A drawing can be zoomed and panned to the spot needed.

![An opened PDF drawing with the share and menu buttons at the top](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/catenda/help-center/main/images/0mhxhsb0/03-open-a-document.png)
